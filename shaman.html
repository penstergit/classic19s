Shaman

As we know in Forever, there are quite a few changes with gearing and talents for Shaman.

However, we are still fulfilling the same role as in Classic and TBC, being a jack of all trades Support.

RACES

Tauren

Best hitbox for horde-side and general jumps. Plainsrunning and War Stomp(now 1min30sec cd) are game-winners.

Plainsrunning, War Stomp, Endurance

Skyborne

Identical hitbox to TBC Belf. On demand 10% speed and 214% speed slow fall are great for movement.

Skysight, Walk on Air, Wind Blessed

Orc

Best (female) hitbox for alliance-side fence jumps. Great defensive and offensive racials with Shatter Curse and Blood Fury.

Blood Fury, Hardiness, Savage, Shatter Curse

Troll

Clunky hitbox, similar to Skyborne. Berserking has a longer duration, you gain 20% health regen in combat, and a 6 second 50% hp bandage every 3 minute that does not share cd with normal bandages. Amazing for self sustain.

Berserking, Rapid Regeneration, Regeneration

Dwarf

Best (female) hitbox for alliance-side fence jumps. Strong defensive racial, but weak offensive racial.

Earth Fury, Stoneform
